# EC014 Metal Hydride H2 Storage — F1b van't Hoff Thermal

## F1b Additions over F1a
- First-order Arrhenius sorption kinetics (Mayer et al. 1987)
- Lumped thermal balance: dT/dt = (Q_rxn - UA*(T - T_amb)) / (m*cp)

## van't Hoff Sign Convention (Sandrock 1999)
ln(P_eq/P_ref) = -ΔH_des/(R*T) + ΔS_des/R
where ΔH_des > 0 (endothermic desorption), giving d(lnP)/dT > 0.

## References

- Mayer et al. (1987). Int. J. Hydrogen Energy 12(11), 753-761.
