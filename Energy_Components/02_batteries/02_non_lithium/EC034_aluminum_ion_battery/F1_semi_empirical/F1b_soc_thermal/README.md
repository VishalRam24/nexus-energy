# EC034 Aluminum-Ion Battery — F1b SOC-Thermal

## Key Physics
- AlCl4- intercalation into graphite; E^0 ~ 2.45 V
- R(T): Arrhenius with E_a = 20 kJ/mol (ionic liquid electrolyte)
- dOCV/dT = -0.0003 V/K (slight entropy decrease on Al intercalation)
- Capacity strongly T-dependent (alpha_c = 0.006/K) due to ionic liquid viscosity
