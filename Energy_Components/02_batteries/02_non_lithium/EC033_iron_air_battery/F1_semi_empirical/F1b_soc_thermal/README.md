# EC033 Iron-Air Battery — F1b SOC-Thermal

## Key Physics
- Fe + 0.75 O2 -> 0.5 Fe2O3; E^0 ~ 1.28 V
- R(T): Arrhenius with E_a = 35 kJ/mol (slower kinetics than Zn-air)
- dOCV/dT = +0.0002 V/K (positive: mild exothermic entropic term during discharge)

## References
